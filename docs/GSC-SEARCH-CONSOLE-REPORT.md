## Executive Summary

Search visibility is small but growing. Over 16 months the property earned **40 clicks, 6,097 impressions, 0.66% CTR, and a weighted average position of 26.85**. Growth is concentrated in October and November 2026: November alone produced 21 clicks and 2,163 impressions by 22 November.

The main problem is relevance, not volume. Most impressions come from SQL-generator queries served by `genielabs.sdad.pro`, not from personal-brand or portfolio topics. The strongest owned page is a blog audit at position 5.54. The homepage is at roughly position 9.5. The projects page has 241 impressions at position 12.59 but zero clicks.

Coverage shows **102 indexed and 128 not indexed** pages as of 16 November. Of the not-indexed reasons, 54 are "Discovered - currently not indexed" and 39 are "Crawled - currently not indexed", so the largest issue is quality or priority, not crawling access.

Structured data issues were fixable in code and have been corrected locally, but they need revalidation in Search Console after deployment.

### Sitemap-specific coverage

A second coverage export was filtered to `https://sdad.pro/sitemap.xml`. It shows only **3 indexed and 29 not indexed** across the 32 sitemap URLs as of 16 November. The only reported reason is `Discovered - currently not indexed` (29); `Crawled - currently not indexed` is 0.

This means Google has discovered the sitemap URLs but has chosen not to index most of them. Internal links matter here. The projects page previously had no links to the individual project detail pages, so those URLs were effectively sitemap-only. Project card titles now link to `/projects/<slug>` to give those pages a real internal link path.

## Conclusion

The domain property is currently carried by three things: a strong benchmark blog post, the homepage, and unrelated SQL-generator traffic from a subdomain. For personal branding, the actionable target is to convert `sdad`, `sai dutta`, and project-name impressions into clicks, then add more pages that rank for category phrases.
